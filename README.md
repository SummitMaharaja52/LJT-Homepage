# LJT-Homepage

Personal academic homepage of **Junteng Liu**.

Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and a member of the HKUST NLP Group. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.

## Research

- Natural language processing and machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

The About page includes his academic background, research experience, publications, and award. The Publications page contains the individual publication records.
